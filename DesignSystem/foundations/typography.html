Typography

Bodoni Moda gives headings personality. Inter does the quiet work of reading.

Two typefaces

Display · Bodoni Moda

Aa Bb Cc

A high-contrast serif that matches my S.Fernandes wordmark. Used for H1, H2 and emphasis only, never below 28px.

Text · Inter

Aa Bb Cc

A neutral sans-serif built for screens. Used for everything people read in long form, plus all UI.

Type scale

A Major Third scale (1.25×) on an 18px base. Sizes shown as desktop / mobile.

Accessibility

  • Body text is 18px with a 1.65 line height, and lines are capped at about 68 characters for easy reading.
  • Sizes use rem units, so text grows when people raise their browser font size.
  • Headings follow a logical order (H1 → H2 → H3), never chosen just for their size.
  • Bodoni’s thin hairlines are only used at 28px and up, where they stay readable.
