Color

A warm, quiet palette: near-black ink on warm paper, with brass as the single accent.

Brand palette

Three brand colors from my S.Fernandes logo kit: Ink, Paper and Brass. Brass is decorative. It is used for fills and large display type only, because it is too light for small text on Paper.

17.1:1 on Paper

3.2:1 · large text only

Accent fills, rules, large display only

Semantic tokens

Components never use raw hex values. They use semantic tokens, which point to the brand colors. The same names are used for the Figma variables.

Accessibility

  • All text colors pass WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1) on Paper and White. Ink on Brass passes at 5.4:1.
  • Brass on Paper is 3.2:1, so it is only used for text 24px and larger, or for non-text decoration.
  • Color never carries meaning alone: selected tags also show a check mark, and links are underlined.
