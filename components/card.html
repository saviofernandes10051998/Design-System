Resource card

The workhorse of the VIA case study: one card for each reflection, icebreaker or tip.

Variants

Guided reflection

Note Who Is Next to You

A short reflection on noticing the people we serve alongside.

  • Solidarity
  • Awareness

10 minGroups of 5–15

Facilitation tip

Immersion Closing Reflection Ideas

Dark variant, for featured items.

5 minAny group size

Anatomy

  1. Type: eyebrow label (Guided reflection, Icebreaker, Facilitation tip…)
  2. Title: the only link in the card
  3. Text: one sentence summary
  4. Pillar tags: 1–3 Pillar tag components
  5. Meta: time needed and group size, pinned to the bottom

Accessibility guidelines

  • Each card is an <article> with a real heading, so screen reader users can jump between cards.
  • Only the title is a link. A stretched pseudo-element makes the whole card clickable without repeating the link.
  • The card border turns Ink when any part of it has keyboard focus.
  • Meta text is 5.2:1 on White. Time and group size are written as words, not icons alone.
  • The hover lift is turned off for people who set “reduce motion.”
